## What this is

Fieldkit v0.10.16 bundles portable Antigravity global hooks (`hooks.json` and `guard.py` v1.7.0) into `fieldkit rules install` and `fieldkit rules check`, enforces Session Mode caps mechanically on Antigravity sub-agent and swarm tools, surfaces an automatic 150k-token context warning advising `/compact` or a new session, and adds multi-agent launch confirmation across Claude, Antigravity, `agy`, Gemini CLI, OpenAI Codex, and Gorilla OpenCode.

## Should you download it?

Yes. Any brand-new or upgraded Windows, Linux, or macOS computer running `fieldkit rules install` (or `install.ps1 -Rules` / `install.sh --rules`) now automatically receives full Antigravity hook protection, 150k context alerts, and multi-agent session choice out of the box.

## Why this matters

Previously, `fieldkit rules install` wired mechanical hooks only for Claude Code, leaving Antigravity users on a fresh computer without `guard.py` or `hooks.json`, without automatic 150k context warnings, and with worker model menus biased toward a single provider when starting remote or local tasks. This release brings deterministic hook installation, sub-agent caps, context alerts, and user-chosen multi-agent launches to every machine.
