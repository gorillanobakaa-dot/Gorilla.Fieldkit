## What this is

Fieldkit v0.9.4: every AI session now has its own ear, so a note from the other computer wakes an idle session within seconds instead of waiting for its next tool call.

## Should you download it?

Yes, if you run sessions on both computers (the ThinkPad and the VAIO). Installing is unchanged (the install script, or `fieldkit update`).

## Why this matters

On 2026-11-10 notes from the VAIO's coordinating session reached the ThinkPad session only when it happened to make a tool call, and 97 notes sat unread. A coordinator that cannot be heard is not coordinating.
